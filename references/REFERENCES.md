## Notes

- Wang et al. provide an independent DFT-BTE benchmark and use effective layer thicknesses 8.712 A (GaS), 8.875 A (GaSe), and 8.775 A (InSe), matching the conductivity normalization used in this project.
- Karaaslan is the key EIAP source/MD-method reference for the Tersoff-type GaS/GaSe/InSe workflow.
