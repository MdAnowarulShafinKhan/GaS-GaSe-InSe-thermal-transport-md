# Licensing status

No formal open-source or open-data license has been assigned to this curated research package.

Until a license is selected, the presence of a file in this public package should not be interpreted as a grant of reuse, redistribution, or relicensing rights beyond rights provided by applicable law.

Before assigning a license, the project authors should:

1. decide separately how original source code and original research data/figures should be licensed;
2. confirm redistribution rights for the included Tersoff/EIAP potential files and any other third-party-derived assets;
3. confirm contributor/coauthor approval where appropriate.

Publisher/literature PDFs from the working archive are intentionally excluded and replaced by bibliographic references/DOIs in `references/REFERENCES.md`.
